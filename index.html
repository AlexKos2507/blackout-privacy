Privacy Policy

BLACKOUT · NeoSite Games

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Who we are

This policy describes data practices in BLACKOUT, published by NeoSite Games. Contact us at neositegames@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Game data on your device

BLACKOUT stores your language choice, completed levels, card-puzzle progress, hint counters and ad-removal status locally on your device. The game does not upload these saved settings to a developer-operated server in the current version. Device or operating-system backups may copy app data according to your device settings. You can erase local game data through Android app settings or by uninstalling the game; this resets progress. Store purchase records are separate.

Advertising and third-party services

The current testing build uses Google AdMob test advertising, including optional rewarded hint ads and ads between rounds. Test ads are not a guarantee that the SDK sends no data. Google Mobile Ads may process IP addresses, device and app information, diagnostics, and interactions with ads to deliver ads, prevent fraud and maintain its services. See Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and Mobile Ads data disclosures at https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure. We do not sell personal information ourselves. Third-party data practices are governed by their applicable policies.

Children and families

BLACKOUT is intended for children as well as older players. In the current version, all ad requests are configured for child-directed treatment with a G content rating, and Android advertising-ID permissions are removed. The game does not ask for a name, email, precise location or date of birth to play, and has no player chat or user uploads. We do not knowingly request personal information from children directly. Parents or guardians can contact neositegames@gmail.com about privacy concerns. These safeguards do not mean that third-party services process no technical data.

Purchases

Ad removal is offered through the platform's store when available. The current test configuration has no enabled purchase-verification server, so purchases are unavailable. The app does not receive your bank-card details; payments and store transaction records are handled by Google Play or the applicable platform store. Before enabling a developer-operated receipt-verification service, we will update this policy to identify its data handling and retention practices. Existing store purchases can be restored when supported.

Permissions and analytics

The app uses internet access for store services and advertising. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or precise location. No separate developer analytics or crash-reporting service is currently integrated. Advertising SDK diagnostics may still be processed as described above.

Retention, security and your choices

Local game data remains on your device until it is reset or removed, subject to operating-system backups. Google and platform stores determine retention for data they process under their policies. Network services use secure connections where supported; no security measure guarantees absolute protection. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data or object to its use. Send requests to neositegames@gmail.com. We can only act on data we control; for store or Google service data, contact the relevant provider. Do not send card details, passwords or identity documents in an initial request.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when app features or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest version. The offline policy in the app describes that app version; the website may contain a newer policy.
